Values are dated observations of the documented connection network as constructed to the stated baseline, not measurements of the coordination landscape. Documented coordination connections overwhelmingly form within industry boundaries in the documented record; the cross-sector share is far below what the corpus’s own industry composition would produce at random.

Documentation accessibility is itself sector-asymmetric — under the coverage-confidence framework the Security domain is graded medium/low, and is additionally constrained by national security classification across all blocs. A within-industry concentration in the documented record is therefore not, on its own, evidence of a within-industry concentration in the world.

Observation Series
Dated observations

Each deploy recomputes these metrics and appends a dated observation. Values are comparable only within a taxonomy epoch: a revision to the industry taxonomy breaks the series, and any trend must be scoped to a single epoch rather than drawn across a break. Successive observations record whether documented coordination remains within industry boundaries or increasingly crosses them as the corpus matures — the series exists to answer that question, not to presume it.
