Documented coordination connections overwhelmingly form within industry boundaries in the documented record; the cross-sector share is far below what the corpus’s own industry composition would produce at random.

Documentation accessibility is itself sector-asymmetric — the Security domain is graded MED/LOW and is additionally constrained by national security classification across all blocs. A within-industry concentration in the documented record is therefore not, on its own, evidence of a within-industry concentration in the world. See the coverage-confidence framework.
